How your net worth is calculated

Everything you own minus everything you owe, counted at your share of each item. Joint accounts count at your ownership percentage. A property counts at your share of its value minus your share of its mortgage. Money you hold for others is subtracted in full.

Between check-ins, values are estimated — carried forward from your last entry or projected from a mortgage plan. The reliability section below shows which is which. Whatever you haven't entered isn't counted: pensions, cars or cash outside the app stay out until you add them.

Mortgage balances are estimates, not lender figures.
